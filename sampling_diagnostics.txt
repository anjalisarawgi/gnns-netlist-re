SAMPLING DIAGNOSTICS FOR GRAPHSAINT / RANDOM-WALK GNN TRAINING
(Complete List for AES Subcircuit Detection Project)

This document summarizes the key statistical measures and diagnostics that can be used to analyze GraphSAINT and k-hop sampling in large graph neural network training. These measures help quantify sampling diversity, bias, effectiveness, and learning dynamics.

1. Importance Sampling Diagnostics
----------------------------------
1.1 Effective Sample Size (ESS)
- Measures how many uniform, independent samples your biased sampler is equivalent to.
- ESS = (sum(w_i))^2 / sum(w_i^2)
- High ESS: sampler covers graph well.
- Low ESS: sampler repeats the same regions.

1.2 Gini Coefficient
- Measures inequality of sampling distribution.
- 0: uniform sampling, 1: extreme bias.

1.3 Sampling Entropy
- H = -Σ p_i log p_i
- Measures diversity of sampling.

1.4 Skewness & Kurtosis
- Describe shape and tail-heaviness of sampling distribution.
